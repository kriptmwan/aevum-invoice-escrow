InvoiceEscrow

tUSD8 invoice escrow on Aevum Testnet (chain ID 44005) — Aevum Builder Challenge #1.

How it works: a payer locks tUSD8 in the contract for an invoice. The payee confirms receipt and the funds release to them. If the deadline passes with no confirmation, the payer can claim a refund. No admin, no fees.
